COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave each calendar year.

Employees receive 12 days of casual leave per year. Casual leave can be used for personal work or short-term requirements.

Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave may be taken when an employee is unwell.

Maternity leave is available for eligible employees according to company policy.

Employees should submit planned leave requests to their reporting manager before taking leave whenever possible.

Emergency leave should be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as possible.
