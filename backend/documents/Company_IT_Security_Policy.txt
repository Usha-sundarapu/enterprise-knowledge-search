COMPANY IT SECURITY POLICY

Employees must use strong passwords for all company accounts.

Passwords should contain a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Employees must not share their passwords with other people.

Multi-factor authentication should be enabled for company applications whenever available.

Company laptops must be protected with approved security software.

Employees must not install unauthorized software on company computers.

Suspicious emails, links, attachments, or security incidents should be reported to the IT security team immediately.

Company data must not be copied to unauthorized personal storage devices.
